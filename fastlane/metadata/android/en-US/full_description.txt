**Galaxy News** is a fast, minimalist RSS reader built for people who want a clean and distraction-free reading experience. Designed with absolute user autonomy in mind, Galaxy News comes with **zero pre-loaded feeds**—meaning you have total control over the sources you follow.

### ✨ Key Features
* **100% Autonomous:** No forced corporate recommendations or default subscriptions. Add only the websites, blogs, and news sources you actually care about.
* **Remembers Where You Left Off:** Never lose your spot. Galaxy News automatically remembers your exact scroll position when jumping back and forth between feeds and articles.
* **Smart Feed Setup:** Easily paste any RSS link with built-in instant formatting checks to ensure your feeds connect smoothly on the first try.
* **Fast & Reliable Images:** Articles load smoothly with intelligent image caching and clean fallbacks, ensuring broken media links never disrupt your reading flow.
* **Distraction-Free Dark Mode:** A sleek, modern interface optimized for comfort and performance across all your screens.
* **Open Source & Privacy-First:** Built to be transparent, lightweight, and respectful of your data.

Take control of your daily news diet and keep your reading fast, private, and entirely yours.
